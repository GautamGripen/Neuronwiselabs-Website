Multilingual AI voice agent

Orateus

Orateus is a conversational voice product concept built to make AI interactions feel faster, clearer, and more natural across languages. It is suited for support, onboarding, assistance, and voice-first customer journeys.

Planned domain: orateus.com

What it can become

Multilingual voice interaction

Support spoken conversations in multiple languages with consistent response quality.

Business-ready automation

Handle incoming queries, route conversations, capture intent, and support repetitive call flows.

Human-like guidance

Deliver clearer experiences for users who prefer speaking over typing.

Potential modules

A voice layer for modern service experiences.

Call handling and smart routing

Qualify requests, provide first responses, and escalate cleanly when human support is needed.

Knowledge-backed conversations

Use structured business knowledge to answer common questions with more consistency.

Analytics and improvement loop

Track call intent, drop-offs, language use, and repeated gaps to improve performance over time.
